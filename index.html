What is a support engineer?

A support engineer is a technical specialist who elevates customer support to an engineering discipline. They combine strong analytical thinking, deep technical proficiency, and communication skills that bridge technical and non-technical worlds. They build workspaces and dashboards, automate workflows, integrate systems, democratize team knowledge, and analyze data -- ultimately transforming support from reactive to proactive.

Does that sound like someone on your team?

We can share our 23+ years of support engineering experience to train and mentor them, helping them quickly master Cerb's platform and implement solutions tailored to your specific needs.

Need technical expertise without hiring?

Our team can fill this role for you. Whether you choose our cloud service or self-hosted solution, our experts can help configure, customize, and maintain your support infrastructure -- giving you all the benefits of our 23+ years of support engineering experience without adding technical headcount.
