Community

Free

  • Add seats as your team grows.

Self-hosted

$35/seat/month
  • Self-hosted on your choice of infrastructure.
  • You're responsible for installation, upgrades, scaling, monitoring, security, and backups.
  • Same business day priority support directly from Cerb's developers.
  • Instant access to the latest stable version.
  • Your license never expires for versions released during your subscription.

Cerb Cloud

$40/seat/month
  • Start with a free trial and no time-limit as long as you're active.
  • Highly available, secure, monitored, and scalable as a fully managed service.
  • Same business day priority support directly from Cerb's developers.
  • Instant access to the latest stable version with same-day urgent bug fixes.
  • Automated, redundant, point-in-time backups with data recovery.
  • Includes Cerb Cloud SMTP service for highly deliverable, high-volume outgoing mail (SPF, DKIM, DMARC).
  • Includes Cerb Cloud MX service for instant delivery of inbound mail (third-party mailboxes are supported but not required).
  • Includes hosting for unlimited community portals (conversational bots, Support Centers).
  • Optionally bring your own domain name (e.g. support.example.com).
  • Switch to annual billing and receive 2 months for free per year.
