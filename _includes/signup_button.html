Self-hosted

  • Installation takes less than 5 minutes
  • Add seats as your team grows
  • Every member of your team can run free local copies of Cerb for testing, development, and staging
  • Downgrade to a community license from self-hosted or Cerb Cloud at any time and retain permanent free access to your data

Cerb Cloud

  • Highly available, secure, monitored, and scalable as a fully managed service
  • No time-limit as long as you're active, and no credit card is required
  • Deploy to secure servers in the United States, Europe, or Asia-Pacific
  • Compliant with EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework (DPF) and GDPR
  • Automated, redundant, point-in-time backups with data recovery
  • Cerb Cloud SMTP for highly-deliverable, high-volume outgoing mail with SPF, DKIM, DMARC (or use your own mail server)
  • Cerb Cloud MX for instant delivery of inbound mail (or check any POP3/IMAP mailboxes)
  • Includes hosting for unlimited community portals (conversational bots, Support Centers)
  • Optionally bring your own domain name (e.g. support.example.com)
  • Switch seamlessly to self-hosted at any time
