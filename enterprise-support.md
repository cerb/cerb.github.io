**Self-hosted Cerb is free to run.** Community installations have the [documentation](/docs/home/), the [guides](/guides/), the [video channel](https://www.youtube.com/@cerb_ai), and the [discussion forums](https://github.com/cerb/cerb-release/discussions/) to draw on, and we answer email as we're able -- no response time is promised, but we'd rather help than leave anyone stuck.

Direct support with a same-business-day response is included with every subscription, self-hosted and Cerb Cloud alike.
