With the [**Calendar**](/docs/calendars) widget, you can display a calendar with events (work hours, holidays, appointments) on your dashboard.

A **card** widget variant is available too, and calendars render through Cerb's own [`CerbUI.Calendar`](/docs/developers/cerb-ui/) component with day, week, month, and year views. Multi-day events render as a spanning strip, and clicking a day creates an event.
